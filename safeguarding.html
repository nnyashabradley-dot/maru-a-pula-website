Safeguarding & Child Protection

Draft outline — not yet reviewed or approved

If a child is in immediate danger, call the Botswana Police on 999 (toll-free) now. To report a concern about a child's welfare, you can also contact Childline Botswana, toll-free, on 0800 300 900. Don't wait for anything on this website — contact them directly.

This page is a general outline only. It has not been written or reviewed by MaP's Designated Safeguarding Lead, and must not be treated as the school's actual safeguarding policy until it's been formally reviewed and approved by the school.

Our Commitment

Every child at Maru-a-Pula has the right to learn, board, and take part in school life free from harm. Safeguarding students — protecting their physical safety, emotional wellbeing, and welfare — is treated as everyone's responsibility at MaP, not just one department's.

The Framework We Follow

MaP's approach to child protection is aligned with the recommendations of the International Task Force on Child Protection, and with Botswana's Children's Act (2009). Botswana has also ratified both the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child and the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child, which set out the broader rights this policy exists to protect.

Safe Recruitment

Every staff member who joins MaP goes through a criminal record clearance and background check before starting work — a standard the school treats as non-negotiable for anyone working with students, teaching or non-teaching.

Raising a Concern

If you have a concern about the welfare of a student — whether you're a parent, a student, a staff member, or a member of the wider community — it should be raised with the school directly and promptly. Once this policy is finalised, this section should name the school's Designated Safeguarding Lead and give a direct, reliable way to reach them.

In the meantime, concerns can be raised through the Contact page, or directly with Childline Botswana or the Police using the details below if the concern is urgent.

External Resources

  • Botswana Police (emergency): 999, toll-free
  • Childline Botswana: 0800 300 900, toll-free — support for any concern about a child's safety or welfare, including abuse
