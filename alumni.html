Once MaP, always MaP.

Maru-a-Pula's alumni network stretches across continents — from diamond boardrooms to concert stages to a former President of Botswana. What connects them is where they started: the same Cambridge academics and weekly service commitment every current student is living through right now.

A network built to last

Over 4,000 alumni, staying in touch.

MaP's alumni community spans the globe, and the school works to keep it that way — a dedicated alumni platform, class agents for individual year groups, and regular reunion weekends that bring former classmates back to campus.

Whether it's updating your details, sharing an achievement, or signing up as a class agent, the alumni office wants to hear from every graduate, not just the ones making headlines.

Pay it forward

OVC Mentorship Programme

Starting at a new school can be difficult, especially for students who feel vulnerable because of their personal or financial circumstances. Alumni mentors serve as role models for MaP's OVC Scholars — students on full scholarships as orphans or vulnerable children — helping them set and work toward personal goals.

Your own experience as a former MaP student is exactly what helps an OVC Scholar make the most of what the school has to offer.
